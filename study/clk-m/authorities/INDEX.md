# CLK-M — Authorities on hand

These are the statutes, regulations, guidance and cases that the `clk-m` Reference Pack relies
on, collected in one place for Drills, mock-1 and the exam's second reference device. Built on
4 Nov 2026.

- **What's in git:** the PDFs here are copied from the library (Nic's Go Pack and Cyril's
  folder) or downloaded from SSO and the Judiciary site on 4 Nov 2026. This folder's
  `.gitignore` keeps them out of git; only this index is tracked.
- **Use the pack first.** Every end point already states the rule with its cite. Open these files
  only to check wording or a section the pack doesn't quote. In a 2-hour, 60-question paper, a
  full judgment is rarely worth the time.
- **"SSO current"** files are the version in force as at 4 Nov 2026; use these.
- **"OUTDATED"** files are library copies that predate amendments. They are kept only because
  the pack's page cites point to them.
