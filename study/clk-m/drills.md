# CLK-M — Drills and Practice Paper

**Drills** (58 Qs) are single-best-answer questions, one set per Subject, written new from the
module materials. The **Practice Paper** is a full 60-question mock, sat with the Reference
Pack. It is PRD §7 item 3 and cannot be cut.

**How to use:**
- Do each drill set **closed-book and timed**, at 2 minutes a question.
- Sit the Practice Paper **with `reference-pack.pdf` only**, in the exam slot (2:30–4:30pm).
- Write your answers down, then mark them against the key after each set.
- Every key line explains why the answer is right and why each wrong option is wrong. Citations
  use the source key in `outline.md`. To log a miss, find its end-point block in
  `flowcharts.md` and that block's coverage Key in `coverage.md`.
- Log scores in the results table below.
- Add every miss, and every failed lookup in the pack, to `weak-spots.md`. Each one leads to a
  chart or index fix.

Self-study only. Don't use these with any live quiz or assessment (Handbook §7.11).

### Drill 1 — Data Protection & Cyber Regulation (16 questions, 32 min)

**DP1.** An online retailer discovers on Monday that a misconfigured server exposed the names,
NRIC numbers and bank account details of 120 customers. It completes its assessment on
Thursday, finding the breach notifiable. By when must it notify the PDPC?
A. Within 3 calendar days of Monday.
B. Within 72 business hours of Thursday.
C. As soon as practicable, and no later than 3 calendar days after Thursday.
D. Within 30 days of Monday.

**DP2.** A breach affects 2,000 customers, but only their names and business email addresses
were exposed, and the organisation assesses no likelihood of significant harm. Which is
CORRECT?
A. It is not notifiable, because there is no significant harm.
B. It must notify the PDPC, but need not notify the affected individuals.
C. It must notify both the PDPC and each affected individual.
D. It must notify affected individuals before notifying the PDPC.

**DP3.** Which statement about a data intermediary (DI) processing personal data for X under a
written contract is CORRECT?
A. The DI is subject only to the protection, retention and certain breach-related obligations;
   X has the same obligations as if it processed the data itself.
B. The DI is subject to all PDPA data protection obligations.
C. Once X appoints a DI, responsibility for protecting the data passes to the DI.
D. The DI must notify the PDPC directly of any notifiable data breach.

**DP4.** Which of the following is NOT excluded from the PDPA's data protection obligations?
A. An individual acting in a personal or domestic capacity.
B. A company incorporated in Hong Kong that collects data from Singapore customers.
C. A public agency.
D. An employee acting in the course of employment.

**DP5.** A Singapore bank wants to use customer transaction data to send customers personalised
marketing messages, relying on the "legitimate interests" exception. Which is MOST ACCURATE?
A. The exception applies if the bank's interests outweigh any adverse effect.
B. The exception does not apply to sending marketing messages; consent is needed.
C. Marketing needs no consent if the customer is an existing customer.
D. The bank may rely on "notification of purposes" as a legal basis.

**DP6.** A customer asks a hospital to delete all her personal data. Which is CORRECT under the
PDPA?
A. There is no right to deletion as such; she may withdraw consent, and the hospital must stop

B. The hospital must delete it, under the customer's right to erasure.
C. The hospital must delete it within 30 days.
D. The hospital must transfer the data to her under the data portability obligation.

**DP7.** An employee of Company A deliberately posts customers' personal data online to
embarrass the company. Which statement is MOST ACCURATE?
A. Only Company A can be liable, because employees are excluded from the PDPA.
B. The employee is liable to a PDPC financial penalty of up to 10% of turnover.
C. The employee may commit an offence of knowing or reckless unauthorised disclosure under

D. The employee's conduct is governed only by POHA.

**DP8.** A former customer sues a company under s 48O for a breach of its accountability
obligations (ss 11–12), claiming anxiety over the loss of control of her data. Which is MOST
ACCURATE?
A. She can succeed, because emotional distress is actionable loss or damage.
B. She can succeed only if the PDPC has not yet made a decision.
C. She cannot rely on breach of the accountability obligations under s 48O, and loss of
   control of data alone is not loss or damage.

annual turnover of $50 million for a negligent breach of the Protection Obligation is:
A. $1 million.
B. Unlimited.
C. $10 million.

**DP10.** Which is NOT an obligation of the owner of designated critical information

B. Carry out a cybersecurity audit at least once every 2 years.
C. Report prescribed cybersecurity incidents to the Commissioner.
D. Ensure the continuous delivery of the essential service at all times.

**DP11.** A cybersecurity code of practice issued for CII owners under the Cybersecurity Act:

**DP13.** X repeatedly sends abusive WhatsApp messages to Y, intending to cause distress, and
