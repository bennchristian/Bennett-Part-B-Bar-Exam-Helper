# DRP-M — Authorities on hand

These are the statutes, rules and instruments the `drp-m` Reference Pack relies on, collected for
Drills, the Practice Paper and the exam's second reference device. Built 9 Nov 2026.

- **What's in git:** only this index. The folder's `.gitignore` keeps the files out of git.
  Bennett authorised the downloads on 9 Nov 2026.
- **Use the pack first.** Every end point already states the rule with its cite. Open these files
  only to check wording the pack doesn't quote.
- **Cases** were read on eLitigation (elitigation.sg) on 9 Nov 2026 and are cited by neutral
  citation and paragraph in each Source note. No judgment files are kept here.

## Read online but not saved

- **CPC 2010 s 16(1)(c)**: read on SSO (https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CPC2010), current version as at
  9 Nov 2026. Used in block 1-10.
- **Singapore judgments**: about 115 judgments were read on eLitigation for the pinpoints in the Source
  notes. Corrections to ARB-SN and MED-3 citations are recorded in `Conflict:` sentences.
- **Not available online**:
  - UJN v UJO [2018] SGFC 16 and Parmod Kumar Verma [2020] SGDC 254 are not on eLitigation; the pack
    uses the course's statement of them.
  - Fasi Paul Frank [1999] 1 SLR(R) 1138 could not be found, so it is not relied on.
- **Foreign cases** (English, Hong Kong, ICC, US, French) are taken from ARB-SN or MED-3 and not
  checked against the judgment. Each Source note says so.
