# DRP-M — Arbitration and Mediation Advocacy (MCQ) — outline and source key

Exam: **Wed 2 Dec 2026, 2:30–4:30pm**. Study block: 13–15 Nov 2026, started early on 9 Nov at
Bennett's request. Built 9 Nov 2026 by the `drp-m` paper session (Phase 1: Markdown only).

## Exam format

- **60 single-best-answer MCQs in 2 hours** on Examplify, about 2 minutes each.
- In 2023, questions 1–36 were Arbitration and 37–60 Mediation Advocacy [PYP23-Instr]. Expect a
  similar split.
- Many 2023 and sample questions use the "I, II, III, IV — which are accurate?" form, so learn the
  limbs of each rule, not just its headline.
- The 2023 paper had no case facts. Questions were short statements of law and practice [PYP23].
- The 2021 papers (ARB, MED) were written papers [PYP21-ARB; PYP21-MED]. They show the issues
  examined (setting aside: arbitrability, composition, natural justice; mediation: Mediation Act
  s 12, ROC O 5, ethics, the seven elements), not the 2026 format.
- Cyril (2025): three questions came from the AA v IAA differences covered in the contact session:
  - stay "shall" v "may";
  - court determination of a preliminary point of law (s 45 AA);
  - appeal on a question of law [CYR-EXAM, "Contact Session details"].
  See Topic 0.II.
- The pass mark is not 50% (Handbook §7.5).

## How the pack is organised

- `coverage.md`: the Coverage List (Subject → Topic → Sub-point), drawn from the Learning Plans and
  Course Materials.
- `cheatsheets.md`: one Q&A Cheatsheet per Topic, then an end-point block per Sub-point, each with
  its rule, a Rule in full box where the rule has limbs, and a Source note.
- `authorities/`: the statutes, rules and instruments relied on (git-ignored except `INDEX.md`).
- `drills.md`, `weak-spots.md`: headings only until Phase 2.
