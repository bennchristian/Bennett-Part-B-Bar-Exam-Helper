# DRP-W-WA Coverage List — Written Advocacy

Paper `drp-w` (DRP written, Tue 1 Dec 2026, 2:30–4:30pm), Subject 1 Written Advocacy. Written
Advocacy has its own Reference Pack and Model Q&A, separate from Civil Litigation (Bennett,
8 Nov 2026). Its build key is `drp-w-wa`. Content IDs keep the Paper prefix: `drp-w-1-<n>`.

**How Written Advocacy is examined.** It is one drafting question (Q5) worth 30 of 100 marks,
typed in its own Examplify window. It took 30 marks in 2023 (PYP23 p8), and by inference in
2025, where Civil Litigation adds up to 70 (PYP25 p2–5). About 36 minutes of the 2 hours.
The document has been different each year:

- 2023: a Statement of Claim (PYP23 p6–13);
- 2024: a letter of demand plus an O 5 offer (SQ p49–55, guide answer p119–121);
- 2025: an affidavit for substituted service (PYP25 p6–10).

The paper prints the rule and the facts. It is marked on substance: "whether your affidavit
contains all the necessary facts in support of the Application" (PYP25 p8 ¶10). Submissions
and a Defence have not yet been set.

`question-bank.md`. `Covered by` = the template block ID(s) in `templates.md`.
