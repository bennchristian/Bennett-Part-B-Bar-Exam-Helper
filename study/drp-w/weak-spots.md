# DRP-W weak spots

A Weak Spot is a Sub-point where a Drill or Practice Paper showed a gap, or where a lookup
in the Reference Pack failed (PRD §3). Each one links to the Pre-write, Cheatsheet or index
fix it led to (PRD FR-10). Sub-point Keys are from `coverage.md`. The main session feeds
open ones into the tracker's `notes` for `drp-w`.
