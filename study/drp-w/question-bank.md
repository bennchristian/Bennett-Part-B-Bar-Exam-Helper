# DRP-W Question Bank — Civil Litigation

Likely questions, by Source Tier (PRD §3): 1 past-year papers, 2 course contact sessions,
3 own questions aimed at under-tested Topics. *Keys* are `coverage.md` Sub-points. Source
aliases are in `outline.md`. Facts are condensed; the model Q&A session transcribes them in
full later.

**Papers held** (PYP index): 2021 CIV (ROC 2014 era, 9 questions, no marks printed);
2022 CIV (ROC 2021, 7 questions, no marks; the text file and the scan are the same paper);
2023 DRP Paper 1 (Qs 1–4 Civil Litigation, Q5 Written Advocacy); 2025 DRP Paper 1 (same
shape); and the 2026 Sample Questions' Civil Litigation question, tagged "[Part B 2023-S]"
(SQ p48, rubric p117–118). No 2018–2020 or 2024 Civil Litigation paper is held. No examiner's
answers exist except the SQ rubric. 2021 and 2022 tell candidates to "always address how
issues of costs … will be dealt with"; 2023 and 2025 say to cite the SCJA, ROC 2021 and PDs,
with no case law needed unless asked.

**The 2023/2025 pattern** (worth knowing in the exam): Q1 service out and jurisdiction on an
Australian counterparty; Q2 pleading a misrepresentation and amending later; Q3 summary
judgment plus one of production, expert evidence or an order to attend; Q4 which appellate
court, permission, then enforcement or a stay.
