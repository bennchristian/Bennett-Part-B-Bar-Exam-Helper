criminal hypotheticals (Q1–Q2, about 30 marks each) and four family questions (Q3–Q6: a short
jurisdiction/divorce question, a PPO question, and two 15-mark questions on children and on

brief to a partner on a qualified plea). Ethics comes up in a criminal part every year.
