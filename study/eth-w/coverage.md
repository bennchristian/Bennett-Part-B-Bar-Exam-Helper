taken from the syllabus (SYL) and checked against the lecture slides and PCR. Source
aliases are defined in `outline.md`.

**Columns** (ARCHITECTURE AD-2). *Tested* = which tier-1 Question Bank entries
(`question-bank.md`) raise it. *Covered by* = the Pre-write ID(s) in `prewrites.md`, or
`item 4`, `TODO`, or `none: <reason>`. Sub-points the past papers test must have a
Pre-write (PRD §7 item 2). The rest are PRD §7 item 4 and are marked "item 4" until written.

**What "item 4" means:** *not covered yet.* There is no Pre-write for that Sub-point, so
the Reference Pack has nothing on it. The label is a reference to the priority list in
`PRD.md` §7, where item 4 is "remaining Pre-writes": Sub-points no 2021–2023 paper or
practice session has tested. They are written only if time allows, after the uncuttable
work, and are the first thing cut if time runs short. As of 3 Nov 2026 no row is
"item 4": every Sub-point has a Pre-write in the pack.
