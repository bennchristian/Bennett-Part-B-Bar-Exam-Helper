### Judgments read on eLitigation (8 Nov 2026)

Where a Pre-write states what a 2023–2026 case holds, the case was read on elitigation.sg,
directly or through an extraction subagent quoting the judgment. Citation corrections found:
*BVU v BVX* is [2019] SGHC 69 (the syllabus prints "SHJC"); *CZD v CZE* is [2023] SGHC 86;
*Wang Piao* is [2023] SGHC 216; *Maag* is [2024] SGHC 311 (the syllabus's [2025] 3 SLR 1093
was not confirmed); *Envy Asset* is [2024] SGHC 38; *Tay Long Kee* is [2000] SGCA 22;
*Lim Hong Kan* is [1992] SGCA 8; the parties are spelt *Xu Xiangrong*, *Tan Hai Peng
Micheal v Tan Cheong Joo* and *British and Malaysian Trustees* in the judgments.
