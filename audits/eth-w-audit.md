# eth-w audit — Ethics & Professional Responsibility (read-only, 18 Nov 2026)

Sources checked: `Bar Exam/Modules/Ethics & Professional Responsibility/1. Learning Plan and Syllabus/`
(Syllabus + Learning Plan, `pdftotext -layout`) against `study/eth-w/` (`coverage.md`, `prewrites.md`
= the Cheatsheets and Pre-writes, `outline.md`, `model-answers.md`, `model-questions.md`, `drills.md`,
`question-bank.md`). Syllabus page references are "Syl p#".

**Core vs non-core.** The 2026 ETH Syllabus has no blue-font / "FYI" tier. I rendered all 13 pages
(`pdftoppm`) and found no coloured text. Its only carve-out is the words "not examinable"
(r 15A–15B family proceedings, r 18A and r 49A–49B funding, r 35–36). Those are excluded here.
So every gap below is **core**.

**Method.** Every case in the Syllabus was grepped by distinctive name in `prewrites.md`
(Cheatsheets + Pre-writes). Every PD/GN, LPA section, PCR rule and LPSAR rule was grepped too.
- **covered** = cited with a holding or use in a Pre-write.
- **thin** = named only with facts and outcome, no usable rule, or mentioned only inside an
  example.
- **missing** = no hit in `prewrites.md`, and none in `model-answers.md`, `model-questions.md`,
  `drills.md` or `pyp-facts.md`.

Counts are of items I grepped. They are not a count of the Syllabus's bullets.
