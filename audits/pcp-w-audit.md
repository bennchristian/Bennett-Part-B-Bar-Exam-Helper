## How the items were counted

- **Criminal.** The Learning Plan (CL-LP) is topic-level and **not colour-coded**: it has no
  core/non-core marking and no reading list with authorities. So the items are (a) the 38 topic
  bullets in the Learning Plan timetable and (b) the 58 distinct cases named on the seven lecture
  decks (pdftotext + case-name extraction, then grep of `prewrites.md`). I also checked every
  statute section quoted on the slides against `prewrites.md`; the only sections not found were
  s 221 CPC (the codified Kadar sections 221A–221D *are* covered), s 451 Penal Code (a sentencing
  illustration) and s 149M CPC (the slide itself says "not in syllabus").
- **Family.** The Detailed Syllabus (FL-SYL, B26S1) has 99 numbered paragraphs. Where one paragraph
  lists several lettered cases (para 58, 64, 67, 83) I counted each case, giving 111 items, plus one
  item from the Learning Plan competencies (forum non conveniens) that has no syllabus paragraph.
  Blue (non-core) paragraphs were identified from the PDF's colour operators, not from pdftotext:
  **[27]–[29], [31]–[34], [36]–[39], [51], [58], [63]–[72], [75], [79], [83]–[85], [98]–[99]** (and
  the "Sections 58 to 67" heading above [86]). Everything else is core.
- **Classification.** *Covered* = a Pre-write / Template states a usable rule, ratio or one-line
  proposition for it. *Thin* = named or cited only (a name in a keyword line or a "further cases"
  bullet) with no rule or ratio. *Missing* = not in `coverage.md` or any block. Past papers held
  are 2021, 2022, 2023, 2025 and the 2026 Sample Questions; no 2024 paper exists.
- This was a presence-and-substance check, not a re-verification of points of law.
