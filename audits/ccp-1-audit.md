# ccp-1 audit — CCP MCQ 1 (Banking & Fundraising · Corporate Governance · Insolvency · Admiralty)

Read-only audit, 18 Nov 2026. Method: 2026 Learning Plans (and, for Insolvency, the *2026 Syllabus & Reading List*) extracted with `pdftotext -layout`; blue font detected by rendering pages and checking word pixels (Insolvency's syllabus defines **blue = NON-ESSENTIAL, "candidates will NOT be tested"**, so its blue items are non-core and not examinable); each item grepped in `study/ccp-1/{coverage,cheatsheets,model-questions}.md`; the end-point block read before calling anything thin. Banking, Corporate Governance and Admiralty plans use no colour coding (Corporate Governance marks sustainability reporting "For Information Only"). Banking's reading list is 12 articles, so those items are checked at topic level, not line by line.

**Bottom line.** The core syllabus is well covered: every Insolvency essential section and case is in the pack, as are all five Admiralty cases and all named Corporate Governance rules except Chapter 12. Real gaps are few: Listing Manual Chapter 12 (a reading-list chapter the pack never read), the Code's provisions for Principles 1, 5, 7 and 11–13, two thinly treated essential Insolvency cases, and the cross-border loans reading. Non-core content is mostly absent, which is acceptable because the plans say it is not tested.
